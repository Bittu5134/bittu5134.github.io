Welcome to another obscure blog on the Internet!
This is the start of my new personal blog:
a personal corner for myself on the World Wide Web!

So why did I suddenly decide to start a blog today, of all days?
Well, once a great man said...

> The best time to start a blog was 10 years ago, the next best time is right now.  
> — Nobody Ever

Jokes aside, I have a tendency to work on random small things here and there and never document them. So I have decided to save all such things here, on this blog.

Going forward, I will be posting all my random midnight projects here.

I look forward to seeing you all around!
